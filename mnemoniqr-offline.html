            How it protects you

            Nothing leaves the device
            Nothing is loaded from third parties, not even fonts, and the security policy blocks every outside connection. Once installed it works offline. A single-file version also exists for devices that never go online.
            Built-in keyboard
            Words are typed on the app’s own keyboard, so your system keyboard cannot learn, sync or log them.
            Argon2id + AES-256-GCM
            The password is strengthened with Argon2id (64 to 256 MiB of memory), which makes GPU attacks much more expensive. Authenticated encryption detects any tampering.
            The QR reveals nothing
            The note, date, passphrase and number of words are not visible: everything is encrypted and padded.
            Shares and decoy
            You can split the backup into several QRs (Shamir): fewer than required reveal no information at all. You can also add a decoy wallet that opens with another password, with no sign it exists.
            Your password is the key
            Whoever has the QR can try to guess it without limit, offline. Use a long, unique password; the dice button generates one of about 120 bits.
            Limits
            JavaScript cannot fully erase memory: close the tab when you finish. Shares are not SLIP-39 compatible. The app language does not change the BIP39 words: you choose those when entering the phrase.
