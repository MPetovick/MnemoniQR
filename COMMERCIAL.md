## Do I need a commercial license?

**You do not need one** if you:

- use MnemoniQR to back up or recover your own wallets, as a person or inside a company;
- modify it for your own internal use, without giving it or offering it to anyone else;
- distribute it, modified or not, or offer a modified version as a website or service, **and** publish the complete source code of your version under the AGPL-3.0, keeping the copyright notices;
- review, audit, study or teach with it;
- read and write MnemoniQR backups with your own code written from the public [format specification](docs/FORMAT.md), without copying MnemoniQR's code.

**You need one** if you want to do any of these without publishing your source code under the AGPL-3.0:

- include MnemoniQR, or parts of its code (for example the encryption, the backup format, the share splitting, the recovery tool or the interface), in a proprietary product: a wallet app, a hardware wallet or its companion app, a custody or inheritance service, a password manager, a printing or engraving service;
- distribute a modified version (in an app store, on a device, as a download) under other terms;
- offer a modified version to users over a network (a website, a white-label service) without giving them its source;
- keep your changes to MnemoniQR private while distributing them.

If you are unsure, ask (see below): the answer is free.

## What the commercial license gives you

Terms are agreed case by case, in a written agreement. Typically:

- the right to use, modify and embed MnemoniQR in your products, and to distribute them under your own terms, **without** the AGPL-3.0 obligation to publish your source code;
- a scope you choose: one product or several, a number of users or devices, a territory, a duration;
- optionally: advance notice of security fixes, support and integration help, and warranty or indemnity terms;
- optionally, under a separate trademark agreement: the right to say your product is "powered by MnemoniQR".

## What it does not include

- **The name and the logo.** "MnemoniQR" and the shield logo are not licensed by the AGPL-3.0 or by a commercial code license. Using them needs a separate written permission.
- **Ownership** of MnemoniQR, or exclusivity, unless agreed in writing.
- **Third-party components** keep their own licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, SIL OFL, CC BY): all of them allow commercial use. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Questions

**Does the AGPL apply to the backups I make?** No. The license covers the software, not what you create with it. Your backups, QR codes and PDFs are yours.

**I run MnemoniQR, unmodified, on my company's website for my customers.** The AGPL-3.0 allows it, as long as you do not restrict your users' rights and keep the source available (linking to this repository and the exact version is the usual way). A commercial license is needed only if you want to modify it without publishing your changes, or for the other cases above.

**Can I sell MnemoniQR?** Under the AGPL-3.0, you may charge for copies or for a service, as long as you provide the complete source under the same license and do not use the MnemoniQR name or logo. To sell it under other terms, you need a commercial license.

**Is MnemoniQR still open source?** Yes. The AGPL-3.0 is an open-source license approved by the Open Source Initiative and a free-software license of the Free Software Foundation. Anyone can read, audit, build and share the code.
